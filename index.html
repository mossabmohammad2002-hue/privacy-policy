Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy explains what information our English-learning app collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. We built this app to help Arabic-speaking learners improve their English, and we take the privacy of that learning journey seriously.

Information We Collect

Account Information

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and display name to create and identify your account. You may also use the app as a Guest without creating an account — in this case, your progress is stored only on your device and is not linked to any personal information.

Profile Information

If you create a public profile, this includes a username you choose, your selected country, and your current level and lesson progress. This information may be visible to other users through the leaderboard and friends features, depending on your visibility settings.

Learning Progress

We store your lesson completion, quiz results, streaks, achievements, and time spent learning, so your progress can be saved and synced across your devices.

Microphone and Speech

Speaking exercises use your device's built-in speech recognition to convert your spoken answers into text. We do not record or store raw audio. The resulting text is sent securely to our servers to be evaluated using AI-based grading, so we can give you feedback on your spoken answers.

Social Features

If you add friends within the app, your country, level, and daily learning activity may be visible to your connected friends. If you enable leaderboard visibility, your username, country, level, and lesson completion count may be visible to other users on the app's leaderboard.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and sync your learning progress across devices
  • To generate and grade speaking and assessment exercises using AI
  • To power optional social features such as friends and leaderboards
  • To improve the app's content and features over time

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase — for account authentication and secure storage of your account and progress data.
  • Google Gemini AI — to grade spoken and written answers based on meaning, not exact wording.

These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy and security standards.

In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

If the app offers a paid subscription, purchases are processed directly by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details — we only receive confirmation of your subscription status in order to unlock the relevant features.

Your Choices

  • You can use the app as a Guest without providing any personal information.
  • You can control whether your profile appears on the leaderboard through the visibility setting in the app.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us using the email below.

Data Retention

We retain your account and progress data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data within a reasonable time, except where retention is required for legal or security purposes.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

How to Delete Your Account and Data

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. To do this, send an email to the address below with the subject line "Delete My Account", including the email address or username associated with your account.

Once we receive your request, we will delete your account information, learning progress, and public profile data (including your username, country, and any leaderboard or friend connections) within a reasonable time.

If you only used the app as a Guest without signing in, no account data is stored on our servers, and there is nothing to delete beyond simply deleting the app from your device.
